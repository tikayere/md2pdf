## Inline Math

The famous identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ connects the five most important constants in mathematics.

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence is expressed as $E = mc^2$, where $m$ is mass and $c$ is the speed of light.

For a normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, the probability density function is $f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$.

## Display Math

The quadratic formula gives the roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

The Fourier transform of a function $f(t)$:

$$\hat{f}(\xi) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\, e^{-2\pi i t \xi}\, dt$$

Maxwell's equations in differential form:

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$$

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$$

## Summation and Products

The sum of the first $n$ natural numbers:

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

The definition of $e$ as a limit:

$$e = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}$$

## Matrix Notation

A 2×2 matrix determinant:

$$\det \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc$$
